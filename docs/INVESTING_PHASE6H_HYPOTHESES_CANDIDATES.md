# Investing Research Phase 6H

Phase 6H materializes only tenant-scoped hypotheses and pre-execution strategy
candidates. Scientific identities reuse the accepted canonicalization and hash
boundary. State transitions append consecutive immutable versions; historical
material is never rewritten.

Hypotheses are limited to `draft`, `active`, and `retired`. Candidates are
limited to `draft`, `ready`, and `retired`. Testing, scientific validation,
promotion eligibility, promotion, experiments, backtests, orders, positions,
fills, accounting, brokers, Trading, queues, and persistent workers are outside
this phase.

The production composition reconstructs scope through the public Investing
identity entrypoint. PostgreSQL composite keys, RLS, and minimal grants provide
independent same-scope enforcement.
